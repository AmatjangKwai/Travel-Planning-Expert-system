Our Story

Proudly Kenyan. Built for the road.

SafiriHub started with a simple frustration: planning a trip across Kenya meant ten browser tabs — flight sites, hotel booking apps, a matatu route group on WhatsApp, and a friend's voice notes about the best nyama choma joints. We put it all in one place.

Our Mission

Make it effortless for both Kenyans and visitors to plan trips within Kenya — real prices in Kenya Shillings, real transport options, no surprises.

Why We Started

From the savannahs of the Mara to the alleys of Lamu Old Town, Kenya's diversity deserves a planner that understands boda bodas as well as it understands beach resorts.

Community First

We partner with local lodges, family-run restaurants, and licensed transport operators so your shillings support communities on the ground.
